Focuser and camera rotator controller for an astrophotography imaging train. STM32G0B1 firmware
with native USB CDC, two TMC2209 stepper drivers, SHT31 temperature/humidity sensors, MPU-6000 IMU
for camera roll and an on-board USB hub. Controlled by an ASCOM driver (Focuser, Rotator,
ObservingConditions) over a USB serial protocol.
