KZ6C is an open hardware six-button USB macropad built around a CH552G microcontroller, with three MX-style keys, three tactile keys and addressable RGB LEDs. The PCB and case are under CC BY-SA 4.0; the firmware, adapted from PadKit and Stefan Wagner's CH552 stack, is under CC BY-SA 3.0. This project is currently a prototype.
