Leif Key is a tiny USB-C key that starts and ends focus sessions on a Mac. It's built
around a WCH CH552T microcontroller; the SDCC firmware enumerates as a vendor-specific
device with no endpoints, and the KiCad board files, schematic and firmware are in the
source repository.
