MIDIMaster is an open-source Windows application for controlling audio and
desktop workflows with MIDI hardware.

Its Virtual Audio component implements a software-defined USB Audio Class 1
microphone exposed locally through USB/IP. It combines a selected physical
microphone with MIDIMaster soundboard clips for conferencing, streaming, and
recording applications. A stable PID prevents the distributed virtual device
from colliding with unrelated USB or USB/IP devices on users' systems.
