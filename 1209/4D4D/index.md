A small open-source 9-key mechanical macropad with an EC11 rotary encoder, built around the Seeeduino XIAO RP2040. Hardware is a CC-BY-SA-4.0 fork of [ANAVI Macro Pad 10](https://github.com/AnaviTechnology/anavi-macro-pad-10); firmware is [QMK](https://qmk.fm) with VIA support. Configurable from any Chromium browser via WebHID — no installs, no account — at [macropad.hrvojematosic.com/configure](https://macropad.hrvojematosic.com/configure).
