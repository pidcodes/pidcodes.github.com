RSH (Real Sim Hub) is an open-source sim-racing wheel: an ESP32 DevKit V1
wheel board (Arduino/C++ firmware) with a Rust + Tauri 2 PC app. This PID
identifies the wheel's USB-serial interface.

Source: https://github.com/dreamsofts34-creator/RSH
