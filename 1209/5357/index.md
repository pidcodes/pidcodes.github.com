The Seven Swords Wedge Keyboard is a 48-key 4x12 ortholinear USB keyboard with a rotary encoder, built on an RP2040. The PCB (KiCad) is released under CERN-OHL-P-2.0 and the RMK-based firmware under MIT. Both are in the source repository.
