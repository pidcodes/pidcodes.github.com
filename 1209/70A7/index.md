The Tuffjoystick is a one-handed analog joystick with an integrated keypad, built on off-the-shelf RP2040 boards (Seeed XIAO RP2040 and Waveshare RP2040 variants) and running open source CircuitPython firmware. It presents a composite USB device (HID gamepad + keyboard, CDC serial for configuration) and shares its firmware architecture and management app with the Tuffpad product line. Both joystick board variants are the same device at the USB level and share this PID.
