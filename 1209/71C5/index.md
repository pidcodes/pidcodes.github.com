A four-channel programmable pulse generator with 250-picosecond edge
placement. Each channel independently produces pulses from tens of
nanoseconds to tens of seconds, phase-locked to an external 10 MHz
reference clock. Channels run synchronously or independently, and each
supports N-cycle bursts. The device is configured with SCPI commands over
USB CDC.
