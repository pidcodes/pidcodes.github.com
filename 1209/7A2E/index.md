A mini [LaserWriter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaserWriter) that renders
pages onto thermal receipt paper. Supports almost all PostScript Level 1
features.

The device is powered by an AT32F435 microcontroller running an embedded build
of my MIT-licensed [TinyPS](https://codeberg.org/Flying-Toast/tinyps) PostScript
interpreter.
