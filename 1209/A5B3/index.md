An open source Pro-micro form factor nRF52840 controller board.

Bootloader uses Adafruit nRF52 Bootloader, and can be found at https://github.com/badjeff/Adafruit_nRF52_Bootloader/tree/master/src/boards/chloroplast

Firmware uses Zephyr or ZMK, and can be found at https://github.com/badjeff/chloroplast-zmk-module
