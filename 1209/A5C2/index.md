YELLOWSTONE is a SAMD21 and LoRa board for tracking and terminating student high-altitude balloon flights. PCB designs (hardware/) and firmware are in the source repository.

The board's USB bootloader (UF2 drive and Arduino/SAM-BA uploads, MIT) is at
https://github.com/Altitude-Unknown/Altitude-SAMD21-Bootloader. The bootloader
and the board's Arduino sketches share this PID.
