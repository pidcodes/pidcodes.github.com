Cordial is a Bluetooth keyboard/mouse to USB HID proxy with accompanying
configuration software. It allows Bluetooth (BLE and Classic) keyboards and mice
to be used in places where Bluetooth devices are inconvenient, such as with
KVMs, in pre-boot environments, or when being moved between multiple computers.
It also handles configuration of Logitech keyboards and translates HID++ keys
such as brightness to standard key codes. The client software is optional after
initial configuration; all settings are stored on the device.

The Cordial firmware is installable on off-the-shelf microcontroller boards with
Bluetooth capabilities such as the Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W, the Waveshare
RP2350B-Plus-W, and the Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3.
