Firmware for a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W (RP2350) or an RP2040 board that scans the keyboard
of a Commodore CBM-II computer (500/600/700). It sends the keys to a Pico64 Keyboard adapter
inside a Commodore 64 (by cable or Bluetooth LE), and on a computer it works as a standard
USB HID keyboard (MiSTer C64 core, PC, Mac) with a CDC serial console.
Built with the Raspberry Pi Pico SDK, TinyUSB and BTstack.
