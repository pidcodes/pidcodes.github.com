Mac Microphone lets Windows apps record from a Mac's microphone. It is a software-defined
USB Audio Class 2.0 microphone (48 kHz, mono) served over USB/IP on 127.0.0.1 only; the
usbip-win2 client attaches it and Windows uses its in-box USB audio driver. A stable PID keeps
the distributed virtual device from colliding with unrelated USB or USB/IP devices on users'
systems.
