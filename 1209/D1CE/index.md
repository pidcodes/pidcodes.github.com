Bluetooth DualShock 4 to wired Xbox 360 (XUSB) adapter with UAC1 audio,
running on the LCTech BL616 (BL616DK) development board. The USB device
exposes a wired Xbox 360 controller through the MS OS 1.0 XUSB10 compatible
ID, plus speaker and microphone UAC1 interfaces. The firmware is GPL-3.0 and
uses a vendored, patched Bouffalo SDK (Apache-2.0).
