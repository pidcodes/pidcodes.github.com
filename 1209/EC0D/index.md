Replacement firmware for the Snowsky (FiiO) Echo Mini 8 GB music player, a Rockchip RKnano device. Over USB it is a bulk-only mass-storage device with two LUNs, the player's internal storage and its microSD card. The hardware is the unmodified commercial player; the firmware is the whole project, written in C and licensed under the GPL.
