GyreFFB is an open-source DIY direct drive force feedback wheel: STM32F405 FOC
firmware for ODESC/ODrive-class motor controllers driving a hoverboard BLDC
motor, plus a desktop configuration app. GPL-3.0-or-later, no serial keys, an
open documented protocol.
