Hrvoje Matosic — solo open-source hardware projects out of Mexico City. Currently building the Matosic Macropad, a 9-key + encoder mechanical macropad designed for AI-assisted coding workflows. Hardware is a CC-BY-SA-4.0 fork of the ANAVI Macro Pad 10; firmware is QMK with VIA support.
